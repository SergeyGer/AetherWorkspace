# Code of conduct

This project adopts the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, specific and evidence-oriented; disagree with arguments, not people.
- Keep discussions focused on the technical scope of this reference implementation.
- Respect the project's data and security rules: no real or customer data, no secrets,
  no uncoordinated vulnerability disclosure.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory or insulting comments, personal or political attacks, public
or private intimidation, publishing others' private information without permission, and
other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that violate this code
and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviours they consider
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. Reports can be sent privately to the
maintainers; they will be reviewed and handled confidentially.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
